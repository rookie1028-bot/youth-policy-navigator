## Field: maintainer role / core contribution

I am the creator and maintainer of Youth Policy Navigator, a local-first open-source web app that helps young people in Korea turn scattered youth-policy information into a prioritized action list. I designed the product concept, KRDS-inspired user flow, seed policy data structure, scoring logic, markdown export workflow, tests, and public documentation. The project started from a real problem I repeatedly saw in youth support programs: people often miss useful housing, job, savings, and education policies because the information is scattered across portals and the next action is unclear. I maintain the repository as an AI-native public-service prototype and plan to grow it into a reusable template for local policy discovery, eligibility triage, and counseling-ready checklists.

## Field: why this project fits the program (max 500 chars)

Youth Policy Navigator is an early-stage public-interest OSS project for AI-assisted civic service navigation. It turns age, region, situation, income band, and keywords into ranked policy recommendations with clear next actions and markdown export. Although currently small, it is built as a reusable template for regional policy packs, public-data validation, and Codex-assisted maintainer workflows. Codex support would help expand data quality, tests, accessibility, and open templates for other communities.
